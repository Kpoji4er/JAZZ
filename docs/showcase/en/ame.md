# African Mercenary Exchange

[Overview](home.md) · [AIM mercenaries](mercenaries.md) · [Русский](../ru/ame.md)

The PDA gets a separate site, **African Mercenary Exchange (A.M.E.)** — not a tab inside AIM. The site chrome uses a **savannah** look (ochre/bronze panels, Africa-shield mark) so it does not read as AIM/HazOS.

## At a glance

- Pool of **60** local fighters; about **15** hireable on the board at once.
- Others are either not listed yet or gone (grey cards: joined the Legion, killed, etc.).
- Market rotates about every **14** campaign days (2 weeks).
- Categories: Irregulars · Fighters · Hardened · Specialists.
- Each slot has a fixed starting kit and its own portrait — no random hire inventory roll.
- Hire week ≈ daily `StartingSalary` × 7: cheapest irregulars from ~**$50**/week, typical ~**$100–$1000**/week, specialists up to ~**$2000**/week — still **below** cheap AIM like Igor/Barry (~$3150–$3290/week).
- Same chat/contract hire path; contract duration slider goes up to **30** days from the start (vanilla capped at 14). A.M.E. hires **do not** consume the AIM contact cap. Board rotation (~14 days) is separate from contract length.

## Why it exists

Long campaigns get a deep local hiring pool for training and squad fill without cloning AIM elites.
