# R.I.S. — Recon Intelligence Services

[Overview](home.md) · [Legion units](legion-units.md) · [A.M.E.](ame.md) · [Русский](../ru/ris.md)

Free in-campaign **intelligence subscription**. Separate from A.M.E. hiring.

## Mail

- Welcome about **2** hours after the desk wakes; PDA tab unlocks when you read the welcome mail.
- Supply briefs ride a **desk queue** (about one mail every **5** hours): starting kit note ~**7** hours (after welcome); later gear jumps ~**5** hours after the change.
- When Legion supply jumps, you get a human brief (what the Major is buying) — not raw tier numbers.

## PDA site

- **Bulletin** — latest supply assessment + R.I.S. mail archive.
- **Dossiers** — Legion fighter types appear in the catalog when their R.I.S. contact note arrives (same desk queue as supply briefs); short cards on how the Major fields them.
- **Battle reports** — fight summaries on the site (no spam mail per fight): **sector name**, weather, intensity, forces, losses; if the fight sits on a **quest badge**, the report names that job and uses quest-linked wording; named elites get their own paragraph.

## Not A.M.E.

A.M.E. is the local hire board. R.I.S. is recon — Legion kit, dossiers, and fight write-ups.
