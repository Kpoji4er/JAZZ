# R.I.S. — Recon Intelligence Services

[Overview](home.md) · [Legion units](legion-units.md) · [A.M.E.](ame.md) · [Русский](../ru/ris.md)

R.I.S. is your field source on the Legion. It does not sell recruits or predict
the future. It gathers confirmed observations, weighs them, and explains what
they mean for the next operation.

The opening message normally arrives after roughly **2 hours**, followed by the
first supply assessment at about **7 hours**. Further dispatches are measured:
no more than one message every five campaign hours.

## Intelligence that earns its conclusions

### Dossiers develop with the evidence

Once a first sighting has been confirmed and its field note delivered, R.I.S.
opens a short contact card with that fighter type's field designation. It makes
clear that one sighting is not enough to assess the fighter's methods. The full
assessment follows after **three confirmed kills** of the same type.

### Battle reports tell the whole story

The archive carries one cumulative account of a conflict, including separate
combat phases, troops already placed in the sector, allies, hostiles, and new
wounds suffered during that engagement. It names the sector, a connected
assignment, and notable enemies when that information is available. If the
objective is won while hostile troops remain nearby, the report says so instead
of declaring the area clean.

Auto-resolved engagements also retain strength, fatalities, and wounded
survivors without mixing simultaneous conflicts in different sectors. The
archive keeps facts rather than a frozen translation, so headlines, place and
assignment names, personal names, and prose are rebuilt in the currently
selected language. Preserved outcomes, assignments, and figures are recovered
from old reports; only missing detail becomes a short localized archive note.

## The Major's Strategy

R.I.S. gradually turns the Legion's observed movements into a wider picture.
Patrols, reconnaissance, transport, retreats, and major responses must leave
evidence before the service draws a conclusion. A hidden headquarters decision
or an unchanged patrol already in view reveals nothing by itself. The first
paper introduces the Major's network; later papers arrive one at a time, at
least **24 campaign hours** apart.

Nothing is shown before the first paper is delivered. Afterwards the bulletin
contains only the material you have actually received, in delivery order, with
no preview of future subjects. R.I.S. explains the Legion's existing campaign;
it does not change its economy or make it act faster.

R.I.S. remains separate from [A.M.E.](ame.md): one provides intelligence, the
other helps you hire people.
