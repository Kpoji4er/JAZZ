# Weapon classes

[Overview](home.md) · [Weapons and components](weapons-and-ammo.md) · [Combat actions](combat-actions.md) · [Русский](../ru/weapon-classes.md)

JAZZ has **eleven** primary firearm classes. Major tiers compare power *inside* a class; classes solve different jobs and are not one linear ladder.

| Class | Combat role | Main strength | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pistol | cheap fast single fire and mobility | low attack cost, solid sidearm | small magazine and range |
| Autopistol | very high close-range density | compact automatic fire | heavy recoil and fast magazine burn |
| Revolver | powerful precise singles and fan fire | high damage/caliber in a compact package | low capacity and expensive reloads |
| SMG | mobile close combat | control while moving, high density | weaker at range and vs armor |
| Assault rifle | versatile squad backbone | single / burst / auto across a wide band | does not beat specialists in their best zone |
| Battle rifle | prepared mid-to-long punch | damage, penetration, controlled pairs | heavy recoil, less mobility |
| Sniper rifle | prepared hits at medium and long range | max payoff from Marksmanship, aim, and optics | costly prep, awkward up close |
| Carbine | mobile mid-range | faster and handier than a full rifle | less long-range power |
| Shotgun | close area damage and assaults | area threat up close | value drops hard with distance |
| Light machine gun | portable support and suppression | long bursts with still-usable mobility | needs Strength, stance, and support |
| Machine gun | hold a lane, max density | strongest sustained suppression | setup, weight, poor control without bipod/support |

Combat actions depend on physical weapon support, the character, components, and perks. Perks can unlock or strengthen class techniques without changing the class’s physical role.
