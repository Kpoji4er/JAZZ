[Overview](home.md) · [A.M.E.](ame.md) · [Mercenaries](mercenaries.md) · [Русский](../ru/merc.md)

The PDA gains a **M.E.R.C.** site — AIM's bargain rival. On day two Speck writes that the office is open again and asks you to find missing partner Biff; that mail unlocks the tab.

## Highlights

- **Credit hire**: chat Offer works with empty pockets; debt on account, settle with **Pay Account** on the site ActionBar (**P**). While anything is due, satellite money grows a **MERC $…** chip (click opens the site), and the same deadline sits on the **timeline** like an AIM contract expiry. After 7 unpaid days Speck writes **and** a popup gives you 3 days; a second popup fires the day before they walk; on day 10 they leave and Combat Log lists who.
- Named Jazz MERC roster available after unlock (Biff not on the shelf yet).
- **Biff, Larry, Smiley** appear only after you meet them in the world (hire on the spot or return them to the site pool).
- Flay and Pierre stay local story hires, not MERC.
- Chrome uses a **JA2 teal** cheap-site look (not AIM, not ochre A.M.E.).
- Loadout shows the AIM-style **Perks** strip.
