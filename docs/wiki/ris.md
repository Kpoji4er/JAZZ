# R.I.S. (Recon Intelligence Services)

Player-facing summary of the free intelligence subscription.

- Welcome ~**2h** after the desk wakes; site unlocks when you read it.
- Legion supply briefs on a **5h desk queue** (baseline kit ~**7h** after awake, after welcome; raises ~**5h** after the jump).
- PDA: Bulletin, Dossiers (contact note unlocks catalog; ≥3 kills for full body), Battle reports with **sector name** and **quest badge** links when the fight is on a job.
- Separate from [A.M.E.](../showcase/en/ame.md) hiring.
